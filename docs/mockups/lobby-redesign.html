What changed, and why

  1. ACollapse the sections. House Rules and Packs become accordions with a live one-line summary (“2 on · win at 7”), so the common case — glance, confirm, start — needs no scrolling at all. Nothing is removed; it's one tap away. This alone is most of the 4,797px → ~900px saving.
  2. BShared Screen Style becomes one row. 1,213px of theme, accent, four sliders, layout and presets collapses to a summary line and a Change… button pointing at the Template Editor at /editor — which already does all of this, better, and is where you'd design a look ahead of time anyway. Removes a quarter of the page and a genuine duplication.
  3. CHouse Rules grouped under subheads. Twelve unrelated toggles become Gameplay, Cards & drawing, Table & pacing. Cheap, and makes the list scannable rather than a wall.
  4. DSub-settings move behind “Configure”. Today, switching on Action Cards injects a density slider and a nine-row tier panel inline; Personal Cards adds two sliders. The page reflows under your thumb and grows unpredictably. A Configure link keeps the rule list a fixed height.
  5. EQuick-setup presets (optional, bigger job). One tap for a whole configuration. This is the real usability win for a party — at 11pm nobody wants to touch 31 controls. The plumbing already exists twice over (Table Style presets, Pack Selection presets), so it's a familiar pattern rather than new invention.
  6. F“Not the defaults” summary (optional, nearly free). A last sanity-check above Start Game. RulesSummaryPanel already computes exactly this for the shared screen, so a compact variant is mostly reuse.

A–D are mechanical: no behaviour changes, no server changes, nothing removed. E and F are separable and can wait.
