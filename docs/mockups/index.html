Table Screen — Big-Screen Mockups

Three directions for redesigning the shared Table/TV view so it's actually readable from across a room, rather than the current approach (the phone layout, uniformly zoomed up). Each one is a real HTML page you can open directly — click through, then say which one to build for real.

Felt & Ink

Evolves the app's existing dark/blue identity for landscape TV — a fanned hand of cards, condensed display type, cool blue accent. The safest, most on-brand option.

Open mockup →

Bulletin

Stadium-jumbotron energy — true black, one hot amber accent, a tabular scoreboard strip, monospace utility type. The boldest, highest-contrast option for viewing at real distance.

Open mockup →

Parlour

Warm, lamp-lit, tactile — an italic serif for the black card (read aloud at the table), cards fanned like a real hand instead of a grid. The warmest, most "physical game night" option.

Open mockup →

Interactive: Table Layouts

A second round of exploration, now that Felt & Ink and Bulletin are live in the real app — this isn't about color anymore, it's about information architecture. Two real layouts (a dense sidebar-scoreboard grid, a persistent status strip) with a submissions area that dynamically fills whatever space is available — bigger cards at low player counts, packing in proportionally as more join — plus an independent black-card size control. (A third direction, a fanned "Distance Arc" hand, was tried and cut here too — same illegible-overlap problem as the original Parlour mockup, even on a real big-screen canvas.)

Interactive: Style Picker

A working prototype of choosing a Table style at room setup — live theme switching, a softer accent-color alternative to Bulletin's amber, a text-size slider, and (since these matter for how well any of these actually hold up) sliders for player count and black-card pick count, so you can stress-test each look at 15-20 players and pick-2/3 rounds, not just a comfy 4-player pick-1 game.
