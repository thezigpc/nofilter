The submissions grid sizes itself to the actual space available: it searches column counts and picks whichever gives the biggest cards that still fit. "Max card size" caps how big that's allowed to get, so a low player count leaves real margin around normal-sized cards instead of stretching them into oversized boxes — check "Unlimited" to compare against the old fill-everything behavior. There's also a legibility floor going the other way (cards won't shrink past ~90px tall); past that it scrolls instead, same as the real app's judging grid already does.
